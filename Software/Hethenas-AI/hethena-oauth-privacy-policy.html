Hethena AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026

Overview

Hethena AI is a desktop operations assistant operated by Intranet Innovations And Services LLC. This policy explains how Hethena handles information when users connect Google services such as Gmail and Google Calendar.

Information Hethena Accesses

When authorized by the user, Hethena may access Gmail message metadata, snippets, selected message content, draft/send functions, label modification functions, and Google Calendar event information. Hethena accesses this information only to perform user-requested assistant functions.

How Information Is Used

Google user data is used to provide features requested by the user, including mailbox search, reading selected email information, preparing or sending user-approved email, moving selected Gmail categories to Trash after confirmation, checking calendar connection status, and showing upcoming agenda items.

Local Storage

OAuth tokens and private configuration files are stored locally on the user's computer. Hethena does not intentionally transmit OAuth tokens to third parties. Users may delete the local credential folder or revoke Google access at any time.

Data Sharing

Hethena does not sell Google user data. Hethena does not use Google user data for advertising. Hethena does not share Google user data except as necessary to perform user-requested functions or as required by law.

AI Processing

When the user asks Hethena to summarize or reason about information, relevant user-provided content may be sent to the configured AI engine selected by the user. Users should avoid requesting summaries of sensitive information unless they understand the configured AI engine and its data handling terms.

Security

Users are responsible for protecting their local Hethena folder, private credential folder, and operating system account. Before redistributing Hethena, users should remove private API keys, OAuth clients, and token files.

Contact

For questions, contact the project operator at the support email configured in the Google Cloud OAuth consent screen.
